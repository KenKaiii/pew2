# GG-style streaming and calmer native UI

## Design read and direction

Mobile remote control for desktop coding agents, not an agent itself. Users monitor answers and approve consequential actions, including over intermittent connections. First glance: answer and actionable state. Second glance: project and context. Primary action: compose or answer an approval.

Preserve native typography, reading rails, Glass press response, FlashList scroll ownership and composer geometry. Use neutral surfaces for provider controls, with a small provider marker and explicit selected state. Keep destructive connection actions one clearly named step away, with their existing confirmation. Preserve warning thresholds and unknown readings. Loading must acknowledge navigation without showing the prior conversation.

Interaction checks: every approval remains immediately actionable, regardless of text pacing; secondary metadata cannot displace the composer or permission controls. Connection controls retain full touch targets and meaningful spoken names. Skeleton, reveal and word fade must respect reduced motion and foreground state. No per-token accessibility announcements.

## Composer and thinking-orb follow-up (2026-09-21)
